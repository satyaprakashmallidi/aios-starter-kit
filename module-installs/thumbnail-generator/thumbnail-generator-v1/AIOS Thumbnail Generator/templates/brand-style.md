## Your Thumbnail Style

<!-- Describe YOUR preferred thumbnail aesthetic. Look at your best-performing
     thumbnails and describe what they have in common. If you're starting fresh,

### Background
<!-- Examples: Dark and moody? Light and clean? Textured? Gradient?
     Describe the overall feel and any signature background elements. -->
- Dark, moody, cinematic backgrounds — NOT solid black voids
- Real-world scenes darkened with cinematic color grading
- Subtle texture and environmental detail for depth

### Person Position & Style
<!-- Where do you typically appear? Left side? Right side? Center?
     What do you usually wear? What expressions work best? -->
- Position: Right side of frame, ~40% of width
- Framing: Shoulders-up or waist-up
- Lighting: Dramatic, natural lighting on face
- Expression: Match the emotion of the video topic

### Typography
<!-- Describe your text style. What fonts (or font styles) do you use?
     How many words? What colors? -->
- Headlines: Bold, heavy, modern sans-serif (ALL CAPS)
- Maximum 3-5 words total
- White text against dark backgrounds
- Use accent color sparingly for emphasis

### Visual Elements
<!-- What kinds of graphics appear in your thumbnails?
     App icons? Screenshots? 3D objects? Charts? -->
- Oversized app icons with subtle drop shadows
- Screenshots or UI mockups when relevant
- Keep to maximum 3 distinct elements per thumbnail

### Composition Rules
<!-- Any specific layout rules for your thumbnails. -->
- Never put important elements in the bottom-right corner (YouTube timestamp)
- All elements must be legible at 320x180px (smallest YouTube thumbnail size)
- High contrast between foreground and background

## Anti-Patterns (Never Do These)

- Solid color backgrounds with no texture or depth
- More than 5 words of overlaid text
- Text that repeats the video title (complement it instead)
- Thin or light-weight fonts (unreadable at small sizes)
- Cluttered compositions with too many competing elements
- Bright, washed-out, or pastel color schemes (unless that's your brand)
