# YouTube Packaging Strategy

> Pre-written framework for YouTube title and thumbnail optimization.
> This is a universal framework — adapt to your specific content.

---

## Core Principle: Want vs. Need

People click on what they **want** (curiosity, entertainment, social proof).
People keep watching because of what they **need** (value, answers, transformation).

Your title + thumbnail must create **curiosity** (want).
Your content must deliver **value** (need).

## 10 Viral Title Elements

| # | Element | Example |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Curiosity gap | "The [X] mistake nobody talks about" |
| 2 | Specificity | "Save [$X] with this [X-minute] method" |
| 3 | Authority | "[Expert name] reveals [X]" |
| 4 | Numbers/lists | "5 ways to [achieve X]" |
| 5 | Timeliness | "[Year] [X] — what's changed" |
| 6 | Contrarian | "Why [common belief] is wrong" |
| 7 | Social proof | "How I [achieved X] in [timeframe]" |
| 8 | Contrast | "[X] vs [Y] — which is better?" |
| 9 | Outcome-focused | "[X] without [common problem]" |
| 10 | Emotion | "[Emotional word] truth about [X]" |

## Thumbnail Psychology

### The Formula
**Face + Emotion + Text + Visual = Click**

1. **Face** — Human face (80% of thumbnails) with expressive emotion
2. **Emotion** — Match the feeling your title creates
3. **Text** — 2-4 words, MAXIMUM. Use contrasting colors.
4. **Visual** — Icon, screenshot, or symbol that supports the title

### Complementary Rule
Title and thumbnail must provide **DIFFERENT** information.
- If title says "5 automations", thumbnail shows something OTHER than a list
- If title asks a question, thumbnail shows the answer visually

### Color Psychology
- **Red:** Urgency, action, danger
- **Yellow:** Curiosity, warning, attention
- **Blue:** Trust, calm, professional
- **Green:** Growth, money, success
- **Black/White:** Contrast, premium, serious

### Composition
- Rule of thirds
- Face in upper 2/3
- Brightness: subject brighter than background
- Text: Sans-serif, bold, high contrast

## 5-Step Packaging Process

1. **Define the transformation** — What does the viewer get/achieve?
2. **Choose the title element** — Which viral element fits best?
3. **Draft 3-5 title options** — Mix elements for variety
4. **Design thumbnail** — Face + emotion + complementary text
5. **Test and iterate** — Watch CTR, keep what's working

---

## Hook Strategy

### The Hook = Title + First 30 Seconds

Your title/thumbnail gets the click.
Your hook gets the watch time.

### Hook Types
1. **Pattern interrupt** — "Wait, before we start..."
2. **Stakes raising** — "This is going to save you [X]"
3. **Tease the ending** — "By the end, you'll know exactly how to..."
4. **Controversy** — "Most [X] advice is backwards. Here's why."
5. **Story hook** — "Three months ago, I made [mistake]. Here's what I learned."
